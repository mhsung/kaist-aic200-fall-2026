Course Description

This course provides a broad introduction to key research areas and modern techniques in AI computing. It is organized into five two-week modules taught by five faculty members, with each module reflecting the instructor's area of expertise.

In each module, students complete a hands-on mini-project that applies concepts from the lectures. Through these topics and mini-projects, students will develop a broad perspective on modern AI and gain practical experience applying AI techniques.
